# Key Messages to Reinforce - Clubs Landing Page

**Purpose**: Core messages that should be consistently reinforced across all copy  
**Status**: ✅ **DOCUMENTED** - Use this guide when creating or updating copy  
**Date**: January 26, 2026

---

## Overview

These 5 core messages are the foundation of Yuvoo's value proposition for clubs. They should be strategically reinforced throughout the landing page, emails, and all club-facing communications.

---

## Core Messages

### Message 1: "Părinți pre-calificați, nu doar lead-uri"

**Full Message**: "Părinți pre-calificați prin quiz AI, nu doar lead-uri generice"

**Where to Use**:

- ✅ Hero section (value proposition)
- ✅ Solution section (4-step process explanation)
- ✅ Benefits section (Founding Club benefits)
- ✅ Email subject lines (lead notification emails)
- ✅ Testimonials (when describing results)
- ✅ FAQ section (differentiation from Google/Facebook)

**Rationale**:

- This is the primary differentiator from Google/Facebook
- Must be repeated strategically to build understanding
- Creates clear value proposition

**Current Implementation**:

- ✅ Hero: "Yuvoo conectează cluburi sportive pentru copii cu părinți care știu deja că sportul tău este potrivit pentru copilul lor."
- ✅ Solution: "Yuvoo filtrează părinții înainte să ajungă la tine"
- ✅ Email: "🎯 Părinte pre-calificat caută {sportName} în {locationName}"

**Reinforcement Opportunities**:

- Add to Trust section: "Părinți pre-calificați prin AI, nu doar lead-uri"
- Add to Pricing section: "Acces la părinți pre-calificați, nu doar lead-uri"
- Add to Final CTA: "Conectează-te cu părinți pre-calificați"

### Message 2: "Zero risc. Zero costuri. Zero compromisuri."

**Full Message**: "Zero risc. Zero costuri. Zero compromisuri. Anulează oricând."

**Where to Use**:

- ✅ Benefits section (Founding Club program)
- ✅ Final CTA section
- ✅ FAQ section (cancellation policy)
- ✅ Pricing section (guarantee)
- ✅ Contact modal (risk reversal)

**Rationale**:

- Reduces objections about risk and costs
- Must be highlighted in multiple places
- Builds trust and reduces friction

**Current Implementation**:

- ✅ Benefits: "Zero costuri. Zero riscuri. Zero compromisuri."
- ✅ FAQ: "Pot anula oricând?"
- ✅ Pricing: "✓ Anulare oricând • ✓ Fără costuri ascunse • ✓ Suport dedicat"

**Reinforcement Opportunities**:

- Add to Hero subtitle: "Zero risc. Zero costuri. 6 luni gratuite."
- Add to Why Now section: "Fără risc. Anulează oricând."
- Add to Contact modal: "Fără obligații. Anulează oricând."

### Message 3: "Doar 15 locuri. 6 luni gratuite. Valoare 594 lei."

**Full Message**: "Doar 15 locuri disponibile. 6 luni gratuite ca Founding Club. Valoare echivalentă cu 594 lei."

**Where to Use**:

- ✅ Hero section (spots counter)
- ✅ Benefits section (Founding Club value)
- ✅ Final CTA section (urgency)
- ✅ Why Now section (scarcity and value)
- ✅ Contact modal (value reminder)

**Rationale**:

- Creates urgency/scarcity
- Highlights value (594 lei)
- Must be mentioned strategically
- Drives action through FOMO

**Current Implementation**:

- ✅ Hero: Dynamic spots counter "{remainingSpots} / 15"
- ✅ Benefits: "Profil complet GRATUIT – 6 luni (valoare echivalentă cu 594 lei)"
- ✅ Why Now: "Primele 15 cluburi primesc 6 luni gratuite (valoare 594 lei)"

**Reinforcement Opportunities**:

- Make spots counter more prominent in Hero
- Add to Solution section: "Doar 15 locuri rămase"
- Add to Trust section: "Valoare 594 lei - GRATUIT pentru Founding Clubs"

- Add to Benefits: "Mai puțin timp pierdut. Mai multe înscrieri reale."

### Message 5: "Break-even: 3-4 leaduri/lună. ROI clar și măsurabil."

**Full Message**: "Break-even: 3-4 leaduri/lună. ROI clar și măsurabil. Economisești timp și bani."

**Where to Use**:

- ✅ Pricing section (value justification)
- ✅ ROI Calculator (interactive tool)
- ✅ FAQ section (ROI questions)
- ✅ Benefits section (when comparing to alternatives)
- ✅ Email (when promoting Premium)

**Rationale**:

- Justifies investment
- Reduces cost objections
- Must be clearly highlighted
- Makes value concrete and measurable

**Current Implementation**:

- ✅ Pricing: "Break-even: ~3-4 leaduri/lună"
- ✅ ROI Calculator: Interactive calculator showing ROI comparison
- ✅ FAQ: "Cât ROI pot obține cu Yuvoo vs. Google/Facebook ads?"

**Reinforcement Opportunities**:

- Add to Hero: "ROI clar: Break-even la 3-4 leaduri/lună"
- Add to Solution: "ROI măsurabil și transparent"
- Add to Trust: "ROI clar și verificabil"

## Message Reinforcement Strategy

### Frequency Guidelines

1. **Message 1** (Pre-qualified parents):
   - Use in 4-5 locations on landing page
   - Include in every email subject line
   - Mention in testimonials

2. **Message 2** (Zero risk):
   - Use in 3-4 locations (Benefits, CTA, FAQ, Pricing)
   - Include in contact modal
   - Mention in email body

3. **Message 3** (Scarcity/Value):
   - Use in 3-4 locations (Hero, Benefits, Final CTA, Why Now)
   - Make spots counter prominent
   - Include in urgency messaging

4. **Message 4** (Value summary):
   - Use in 3-4 locations (Problem, Solution, Testimonials)
   - Include in Hero subtitle
   - Mention in email body

5. **Message 5** (ROI):
   - Use in 2-3 locations (Pricing, ROI Calculator, FAQ)
   - Highlight in Benefits section
   - Include in Premium promotion emails

### Placement Priority

**High Priority Locations** (Must include):

- Hero section
- Final CTA section
- Benefits section
- Email subject lines

**Medium Priority Locations** (Should include):

- Solution section
- Pricing section
- FAQ section
- Testimonials

**Low Priority Locations** (Can include):

- Footer
- Trust section
- Why Now section

## Implementation Checklist

When creating or updating copy, ensure:

- [ ] At least 3-4 core messages are present on landing page
- [ ] Message 1 (pre-qualified) appears in Hero
- [ ] Message 2 (zero risk) appears in Benefits/CTA
- [ ] Message 3 (scarcity/value) appears in Hero/Final CTA
- [ ] Message 4 (value summary) appears in Problem/Solution
- [ ] Message 5 (ROI) appears in Pricing/FAQ
- [ ] Messages are varied (not repetitive)
- [ ] Messages align with brand voice (70% warm, 30% expert)
- [ ] All messages are in Romanian

---

## Brand Voice Alignment

All messages must maintain Yuvoo brand voice:

- **70% Warm, Supportive, Empathetic**
- **30% Confident, Expert, Professional**
- **Tone**: "Profesional, dar cald. Inteligent, dar uman."

**Examples**:

- ✅ "Părinții care știu deja ce caută" (warm, empathetic)
- ✅ "ROI clar și măsurabil" (expert, professional)
- ✅ "Zero risc. Zero costuri." (confident, clear)
