# Yuvoo Legal Compliance Checklist (1 Page)

**Reference:** Full analysis in `LEGAL_ANALYSIS_YUVOO_COMPREHENSIVE.md`  
**Scope:** Pre-launch MVP (Romania/EU); child + health data + AI

---

## Before Launch (Must-Haves)

- [ ] **Legal basis:** Document Art. 6 (consent/contract) and Art. 9(2)(a) (explicit consent) for all child and health data; state in Privacy Policy.
- [ ] **Consent:** Explicit consent for health data; parental consent for child data; separate purposes; no pre-ticked boxes.
- [ ] **Consent records:** Log who, when, what, policy version; retain ≥6 years.
- [ ] **DPIA:** Complete DPIA (children + health + automated decisions); implement mitigations; set review trigger.
- [ ] **Privacy Policy:** Purposes, legal basis, health data, AI/Art. 22, transfers (OpenAI/SCCs), retention, rights (access, erasure, portability, withdrawal, complaint), contact.
- [ ] **DPAs:** Signed with OpenAI, Neon, Cloudinary, Brevo (+ any other processors); SCCs; OpenAI: no training on customer data; US transfer TIA if applicable.
- [ ] **Rights:** Access, rectification, erasure, portability, withdrawal, objection; human intervention for automated decisions.
- [ ] **Clubs/doctors:** Define controller/processor; contracts or notices; share only per consent.
- [ ] **Security:** Encryption (transit + rest), access controls, minimal access to child health data.
- [ ] **Breach process:** 72h notification to ANSPDCP; notify individuals when high risk.
- [ ] **Disclaimers:** “Not medical advice”; “consult a healthcare professional”; “recommendations are indicative.”

---

## Post-Launch (Ongoing)

- [ ] **Art. 30:** Records of Processing up to date.
- [ ] **Consent & breach registers:** Maintain and review.
- [ ] **DPIA:** Re-run when adding data/purposes or changing AI.
- [ ] **Subprocessors & transfers:** Periodic review; update TIAs/contracts.
- [ ] **AI Act / MDR:** Monitor applicability (e.g. Aug 2026); implement if high-risk or medical device.

---

## Nice-to-Haves

- [ ] **DPO:** Designate if large-scale special-category or systematic monitoring (Art. 37).
- [ ] **ISO 27001 / 27701:** When scaling or required by partners.
- [ ] **External audit:** For enterprise or regulatory assurance.
