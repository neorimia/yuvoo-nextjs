# Romanian Pricing Research Summary

## Premium Club Subscription: 99 lei/month with Annual Discount

**Research Date**: January 2026  
**Recommendation**: **Monthly Base with Annual Discount** (High Confidence)

---

## Executive Summary

After comprehensive research of the Romanian B2B SaaS market, sports club industry, and payment behavior patterns, the recommendation is to **offer monthly subscription at 99 lei/month as base price, with annual option at 990 lei/year (equivalent to 10 months, get 2 months free - 17% discount)**.

### Key Findings

1. **Market Alignment**: Romanian B2B SaaS platforms consistently offer both monthly and yearly options with 14-20% annual discounts
2. **Cash Flow Reality**: 85% of Romanian SMEs cite poor cash flow as cause of business failure; prefer flexible payment terms
3. **Payment Preferences**: 55% of B2B transactions are cash-based; average payment terms are 36 days
4. **Value Perception**: 99 lei/month sits below 100 lei psychological threshold, making it highly accessible. Annual discount (17%) aligns with market standards while providing clear value proposition

## Market Analysis

### Romanian B2B SaaS Pricing Patterns

**Typical Pricing Ranges:**

- **Infrastructure SaaS** (Netart.com): 100-400 RON/month, with ~14% annual discount
- **Business SaaS** (Banqup): 12.50-912 RON/month, with 20% annual discount
- **Productivity SaaS** (Microsoft 365): 48-110 RON/month, or 485-1,100 RON/year
- **Marketing SaaS** (BRILU - Romanian): 99-279€/month (~500-1,400 RON), or 790-2,290€/year

**Key Insight**: Annual subscriptions with 14-20% discounts are standard. Yuvoo's 17% annual discount (990 lei/year vs 1,188 lei/year monthly) aligns with market expectations while providing clear value proposition - pay for 10 months, get 2 months free.

### Sports Club Industry Context

- **Financial Structure**: Many clubs are small businesses/NGOs with limited budgets
- **Cash Flow Challenges**: 85% of SMEs cite poor cash flow management as failure cause
- **Marketing Spend**: Limited budgets, focus on social media and sponsorships
- **Payment Behavior**: Prefer cash payments and flexible terms to reduce liquidity risks

### Competitive Landscape

**Direct Competitors**: Limited

- VreaULeaduri.ro (B2B lead gen, pricing not public)
- Lead Mate (15-30+ leads/month, monthly model)
- GIKO Romania (14-day trial, subscription-based)
- SellNet/RISCO (database access, free trial)

**Market Gap**: Most platforms focus on B2B sales leads, not B2C parent leads for sports clubs. Yuvoo's pre-qualified parent leads are unique.

## Pricing Analysis

### Monthly Model: 99 lei/month - **BASE PRICE**

**Pros:**

- ✅ **Below 100 lei psychological threshold** (stronger than 199 lei)
- ✅ Lower barrier to entry
- ✅ Better cash flow for clubs (monthly payments)
- ✅ Competitive with market entry-level SaaS (Microsoft 365: 48 RON, Netart: 100 RON)
- ✅ Break-even at ~3.3 leads/month vs pay-per-lead (3€ = ~15 lei per lead)
- ✅ More accessible for cash-strapped clubs
- ✅ Aligns with Romanian preference for cash/flexible payments
- ✅ Easier to test service before annual commitment
- ✅ No upfront commitment required

**Cons:**

- ❌ Higher churn risk (monthly cancellations easier)
- ❌ More payment processing overhead
- ❌ Requires consistent monthly value delivery
- ❌ Lower customer lifetime value if clubs churn quickly

**Impact**: Positive conversion, Negative retention

### Annual Model: 990 lei/year (82.5 lei/month equivalent) - **RECOMMENDED FOR VALUE**

**Pros:**

- ✅ Clear value proposition: Pay for 10 months, get 2 months free
- ✅ 17% discount vs monthly (1,188 lei/year vs 990 lei/year)
- ✅ Aligns with market annual discount pattern (14-20%)
- ✅ Reduces payment friction (single annual transaction)
- ✅ Better cash flow for Yuvoo (upfront payment)
- ✅ Lower churn risk (annual commitment)
- ✅ Equivalent to 82.5 lei/month (vs 99 lei/month)
- ✅ Saves 198 lei/year compared to monthly

**Cons:**

- ❌ Higher upfront cost (990 lei vs 99 lei)
- ❌ May be barrier for cash-strapped clubs
- ❌ Requires budget certainty

**Impact**: Positive conversion, Positive retention

### Break-Even Analysis

- **Monthly (99 lei/month)**: Requires 3.3 leads/month to justify cost vs pay-per-lead (3€ = ~15 lei per lead)
- **Annual (990 lei/year = 82.5 lei/month)**: Requires 2.75 leads/month to justify cost vs pay-per-lead
- **Value Proposition**:
  - For clubs receiving 3+ leads/month: Monthly (99 lei) provides value
  - For clubs receiving 3+ leads/month: Annual (990 lei/year) provides better value (17% discount)
  - Annual savings: 1,188 lei/year (monthly) vs 990 lei/year (annual) = 198 lei savings (2 months free)

## Psychological Pricing Factors

### Psychological Pricing Thresholds

**Monthly (99 lei/month) - Base Price**: Sits **below 100 lei psychological threshold**, which is a powerful conversion driver. Creates perception of being in "under 100" category, making price appear significantly more affordable. This threshold is more powerful for conversion than the 200 lei threshold.

**Annual (990 lei/year = 82.5 lei/month)**: Equivalent monthly cost of 82.5 lei is well below 100 lei threshold, reinforcing value perception. The 990 lei annual price sits just below 1,000 lei psychological threshold, making it appear more affordable than 1,000+ alternatives.

**Value Perception**: High for both models. The 99 lei/month base price balances accessibility (below 100 threshold) with value perception, while annual provides clear savings (17% discount, 2 months free) for clubs with budget certainty.

### Payment Behavior

- **Cash Preference**: 55% of B2B transactions are cash-based
- **Payment Terms**: Average 36 days from invoicing
- **Cash Flow Concerns**: Critical for 85% of SMEs
- **Commitment Level**: Annual requires higher commitment but better value; monthly reduces barrier but increases cancellation risk

## Recommendations

### Primary Recommendation: Monthly Base with Annual Discount

**Offer Both Options** with clear positioning:

1. **Monthly (99 lei/lună)** - "Base Price" / "Flexible"
   - Position as: "Plată flexibilă lunară"
   - Highlight: "Doar 99 lei/lună - sub pragul de 100 lei"
   - Emphasize: "Anulează oricând" (Cancel anytime)
   - Show: "Break-even la ~3-4 leaduri/lună"
   - Add: 7-14 day free trial
   - No upfront commitment

2. **Annual (990 lei/an)** - "Best Value" / "Recommended"
   - Position as: "Economisește 198 lei/an" (Save 198 lei/year: 1,188 lei - 990 lei)
   - Highlight: "Plătești pentru 10 luni, primești 12" (Pay for 10 months, get 12)
   - Show: "Doar 82.5 lei/lună echivalent" (Only 82.5 lei/month equivalent)
   - Emphasize: "17% reducere" (17% discount)
   - Add: "Most Popular" badge

**Rationale**: The 99 lei/month base price balances accessibility (below 100 psychological threshold), market competitiveness (aligns with entry-level SaaS), and value perception (break-even at ~3.3 leads/month). Annual option provides clear value (17% discount, 2 months free) that aligns with market standards (14-20% discounts) while remaining accessible. This model accommodates different cash flow preferences while maintaining clear value proposition for both options.
